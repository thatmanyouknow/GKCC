Blobifier commissioning

Use the hardware you already installed as the first complete GKCC workflow test: inspect, import, measure, dry-run, create a supervised blob, tune, back up, apply, and verify.

Stay at the printer: Blobifier can move the toolhead to the rear-left edge, lower Z near the tray, operate a servo, heat the nozzle, and extrude filament. Keep the bed and motion envelope clear and keep emergency stop ready for every first-run action.

Teach a machine location

Home the printer, use the slow jog controls to place the nozzle exactly where the hardware is, then capture the live G-code coordinates. GKCC can write the measured location into the draft configuration and immediately highlight the affected lines.

Supervised movement only. Start several millimetres above the hardware, use large steps only in open space, then reduce to 1.0, 0.1, or 0.01 mm near a brush, dock, tray, bed, or probe. GKCC blocks jogging during a print, requires XYZ homed, and checks Klipper travel limits.

Commissioning order

Hardware and wiring → official files → switch → servo endpoints → collision envelope → measured geometry → dry brush/shake → small heated blob → tuning → repeated cycles → backed-up live apply → real tool change.

Do not skip directly to the heated blob. Each completed step records measurements and notes so the workflow itself can be improved from this real installation.

Step-by-step commissioning path

1. Servo-only tray test

These commands only operate the Blobifier servo macro, but the tray and linkage can still pinch, bind, or collide. Keep fingers clear.

Adjust pulse widths and angles until both end positions are complete without buzzing or forcing the linkage. Do not use a buzzing servo as an endstop.

2. Supervised dry movement

Start with deliberately high brush clearance and only a few shakes. Lower or increase motion one measured step at a time.

3. First supervised test blob

This command may home/check motion state, move to the Blobifier, heat to the configured minimum, extrude, operate the tray, and brush the nozzle. Load suitable filament and verify the bucket is installed before continuing.
